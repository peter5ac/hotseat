Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Do I need an internet connection to play?

A: No! Hotseat is designed for local pass-and-play gameplay and works completely offline.

Q: How many players does Hotseat support?

A: Player limits depend on the specific game mode selected inside the app.

Q: Does the app save player names or scores across restarts?

A: All game data and settings are stored locally on your device.
