Privacy Policy for Hotseat

Effective Date: October 2026

Your privacy is paramount. This Privacy Policy explains how Hotseat handles your information.

1. Information Collection and Use

Hotseat does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal data or personally identifiable information.

The app operates completely offline and locally on your device. Nothing is saved between sessions, and nothing is ever uploaded to external servers. There are no accounts, and Hotseat does not record, photograph, or share anything.

2. Tracking & Analytics

Hotseat does not use any third-party tracking services, advertising networks, or telemetry analytics SDKs. We do not track your activity across other apps or websites.

3. Permissions

Hotseat asks for the following permissions. All processing happens live on your device, and no images, video, or audio are ever recorded, stored, or transmitted.

  • Camera: the front camera is shown as a live background and used for face-based games. Video is never recorded or saved.
  • Microphone: used only by the Vocal Glide game to detect the pitch and volume of your voice in real time. Audio is never recorded or saved.
  • Motion sensors: used only for gameplay, such as tilting the device.

Hotseat does not request access to your location, contacts, photos, or any other personal data.

4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. However, as we do not collect user data, our core commitment to user privacy will remain unchanged. Any updates will be posted on this page.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please visit our Support Page.
